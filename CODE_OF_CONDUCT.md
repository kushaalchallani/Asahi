# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, sexual identity, and religion.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for all participants include:

- **Being friendly and patient** - Welcome newcomers and be patient with those who are less experienced.
- **Being respectful** - Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- **Being constructive** - Focus on what's best for the community.
- **Being careful in your criticism** - Offer constructive feedback, not condemnation.
- **Being graceful** - Gracefully accept constructive feedback and thank people for their input.
- **Focusing on what's best** for this community, not just for a particular follower or group.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment** in any form.
- **Discriminatory jokes and language**.
- **Public or private discrimination**.
- **Trolling** and **insulting/derogatory comments**.
- **Publishing others' private information** such as physical addresses or email accounts without explicit permission.
- **Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting**.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at [your-email@example.com]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. Project maintainers who have received complaints that a participant has violated this Code of Conduct may temporarily or permanently ban such participants from the project and its associated spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community. Examples of representing the project include using a project email address, posting via a social media account dedicated to the project, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

This Code of Conduct is not intended to limit the right of anyone to freely express themselves outside of project spaces, provided that such expression does not rise to the level of harassment or discrimination as described above.
